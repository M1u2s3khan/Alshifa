About the Centre

DR. (Hkm) Alshifa Healthcare Unani Medical Centre was established in 2020 in Gulaohi, New Delhi with a mission – to prevent and treat various disease conditions / health problems through a holistic Unani approach.

Unani medicine uses various natural herbs and therapies with minimal side effects. The centre provides expert Unani consultation and quality Unani medicines at the convenience of the patients.

The centre was founded by India's renowned Unani physician ‘DR. Mohammed Deen’, who has 15 years of rich experience and has successfully practiced at Tibia College, Karol Bagh and later at Majidia Hospital, Jamia Hamdard, New Delhi.

Explore our traditional Unani regimenal therapies – each treatment is rooted in centuries of wisdom.

Hijama Cupping Therapy

In Hijama, cups are applied on specific points with negative pressure to draw out blood. It improves circulation and relieves pain.

Hijama Treatment Session

In a professional Hijama session, the patient is placed comfortably and the expert practitioner performs cupping.

Fasd (Venesection)

Fasd involves drawing a specific amount of blood from veins, which reduces blood pressure and inflammation.

Fasd Bloodletting

This ancient Unani technique restores balance by removing excess morbid blood from the body.

Irsale Alaq (Leech Therapy)

Medicinal leeches are applied to the affected area; they suck blood and reduce inflammation.

Leech Therapy

Enzymes in leech saliva thin the blood and provide relief from local pain.

Dalk (Massage)

Dalk involves body massage with special oils, which relaxes muscles and improves blood flow.

Dalk Oil Massage

Dalk is highly beneficial for arthritis, back pain and joint stiffness.
